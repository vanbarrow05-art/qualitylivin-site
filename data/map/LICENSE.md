# Map data licence

`data/map/` is a derived database of public business listings used by the provider map on qualitylivin.com.

- Contains information from OpenStreetMap, which is made available under the Open Database License (ODbL) 1.0: https://www.openstreetmap.org/copyright
- Contains Overture Maps Foundation places data, licensed under CDLA-Permissive-2.0: https://overturemaps.org
- ZIP centroids: U.S. Census Bureau ZCTA Gazetteer (public domain).

This derived database (`states/*.json`, `summary.json`) is made available under the ODbL 1.0.

The listed businesses have not joined or been reviewed by Quality Living. Pins sit near each business's ZIP centre, not its street address. No phone numbers, emails or street addresses are included.

Quality Living's own partners live in `data/providers.csv` (status `joined`). Add a business there only after it agrees in writing to be listed.
