# Print launch plan: physical Quality Living Magazine by January 1, 2027

Today is **October 23, 2026**, which leaves **10 weeks**. That's enough for a first print issue if the content work starts now and you use a printer that can turn around in under 2 weeks. December shipping is slow, so the plan targets **printed copies in hand by December 18**.

## Fix these three things first (they block printing)

1. **The article text is placeholder.** Headlines and photos work, but the body copy in the April 2025 PDF is jumbled filler ("Eathawul sets natural benefits…"). Every article needs real, edited text before print. This is the biggest job.
2. **The subscription offer doesn't add up.** The back cover sells **12 issues + free delivery for $39.95/year** (about $3.33 an issue). A short-run printed magazine typically costs roughly **$3–8 per copy to print** and **$1–3 to mail**, so every printed issue would lose money. Fix it before selling subscriptions, for example:
   - **Quarterly print + monthly digital** for $39.95/year (4 printed issues), or
   - **12 printed issues at ~$79–99/year**, or
   - **$39.95/year digital**, with print as an add-on.
3. **Remove the fake barcode and price box** from the cover unless you register them. A retail barcode needs a real GS1/UPC number, which you only need for newsstand sales. Direct and local distribution doesn't need one.

## Optional (after issue 1)

- **ISSN:** free from the Library of Congress for serials; makes the magazine look established and helps libraries/catalogs. Apply once the first issue is published.
- **Newsstand / retail:** needs a UPC barcode (GS1), a distributor, and returns handling. Not worth it until you have steady circulation.
- **Postal periodicals rate:** cheaper mailing for paid subscribers once you have a regular schedule and enough subscribers. Ask USPS about Periodicals eligibility.
