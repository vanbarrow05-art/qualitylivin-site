# 1. The partner offer and packages

This is the offer every caller should be able to explain in 30 seconds. Prices and names match the website's "For service providers" section. If you change them here, change them on the site too, and vice versa.

## The ladder

| Step | What the provider gets | What they pay |
|---|---|---|
| **1. Three free leads** | 3 genuine service inquiries matched to their services and territory. Invalid or duplicate leads don't count toward the 3. | Nothing. No card, no contract, no referral fee on those 3. |
| **2. Referral Partner** | Ongoing leads after the free 3, sent as we get them. | $0/month + **10% of eligible collected revenue** from Quality Living referrals. On a $1,000 job collected, the fee is $100. |
| **3. Directory Presence** | Approved profile on the Quality Living map and directory, service areas, portfolio, review-source links, inquiry routing + booking link, monthly activity summary, assigned Partnerships Manager. | **$400 / month** |
| **4. AI Autopilot & Growth** | Everything above + managed website, AI phone receptionist and email handling, estimate booking/reminders, missed-inquiry follow-up, local SEO, **scheduled social posts** (3–5/week across their platforms), monthly reporting. | **$800 / month** |
| **5. Scale & Media Partner** | Everything above + up to 2 locations/territories, short videos and original content, review-request and repeat-booking workflows, bi-weekly performance reviews, priority support, **magazine feature**. | **$1,600 / month** |
| Add-on: **Meta ads** | Paid campaigns with an approved budget, cap, schedule and territory. | Ad spend from **$75/day**, funded separately. |

**The 10–20% question:** the site currently says 10%. If you want 15–20% for higher-ticket trades (construction, remodeling), put that in each partner's agreement *and* update the site wording to "10–20% depending on trade", so the site never promises a lower number than the contract.

## Who we offer it to, in order

1. **Janitorial & cleaning (now):** residential cleaning, commercial janitorial, short-term rental turnovers, move-in/out, deep cleaning.
2. **Construction & remodeling, plumbing (next):** higher ticket, so the 10–20% referral fee is worth more to us.
3. **Massage & wellness, interior designers (later):** these fit the magazine audience; sell magazine features + Scale & Media.

## Which providers qualify ("5-star")

We only recruit and list providers who meet **all** of these. Write the evidence into the CRM.

- **Rating:** 4.7★ or higher on Google, *and* not below 4.5 on any other major site we check (Yelp, Trustpilot, BBB, Angi).
- **Volume:** 40+ Google reviews (residential), 20+ (commercial janitorial, which gets fewer reviews), with reviews in the last 90 days.
- **Licensed & insured:** proof of general liability insurance; business license / contractor license where the trade requires it (construction, plumbing, electrical, HVAC: check the state license lookup).
- **Responsive:** answers or returns a call within 1 business day during prospecting. If they don't answer us, they won't answer our leads.
- **No red flags:** no unresolved BBB complaints pattern, no review-gating or obviously fake reviews.

## Where the money comes from (unit math to check before you scale)

- **Free leads cost us money.** Each lead we generate has an ad cost. Track **cost per lead (CPL)** from day one. If a lead costs $25, giving 3 free leads to 40 providers is $3,000 of leads before revenue.
- **Referral revenue only counts when it's collected.** You need the provider to report jobs, or a booking/payment integration that shows them. Put reporting duties in the agreement.
- **The packages are where margin is.** Goal: 1 in 5 providers who receive free leads upgrades to a paid plan within 60 days.

## What we never say

- That a provider is "in the network", "verified", or "approved" before they've agreed in writing and passed the checks above.
- A rating we haven't seen ourselves, or one without its source and date.
- A guaranteed number of leads, jobs or revenue. The site says "no guarantee shown". Keep it that way.
