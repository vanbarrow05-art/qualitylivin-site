# 5. Partner agreement: outline (one page)

*An outline to give an attorney, not a finished contract. Keep the final version to one page plus a short terms page so owners actually read it.*

**Parties:** Quality Living ("we") and [Company], [address] ("Partner").

1. **Free pilot.** We'll send Partner up to **3 free leads** matched to Partner's services and service area. No fee applies to these 3. Invalid leads (fake contact details, duplicates, outside the service area, or services Partner doesn't offer) don't count and will be replaced.
2. **What a lead is.** A customer inquiry submitted through Quality Living with name, contact details, service, location and timing, sent to Partner only (not shared with competing providers).
3. **After the pilot.** Partner chooses:
   - **Referral Partner:** we keep sending leads; Partner pays **[10]% of revenue collected** from customers we referred, for **[12] months** from each customer's first job (including repeat jobs from that customer in that window).
   - **Monthly plan:** Directory Presence $400, AI Autopilot & Growth $800, or Scale & Media Partner $1,600 per month, scope per the plan sheet. While on a monthly plan, the referral % [does / does not] apply.
4. **Reporting & payment (Referral Partner).** Partner reports jobs booked from our leads by the **5th of each month** (or connects their booking system), and pays the fee within 15 days of our invoice. We may ask for reasonable records to confirm.
5. **Listing consent.** Partner agrees we may show its business name, logo, services, service areas, contact/booking links and public review ratings (with source) on qualitylivin.com, in the directory/map, and [in Quality Living Magazine]. Partner can withdraw consent at any time by email; we'll remove the listing within 5 business days.
6. **Partner promises.** Licensed and insured as required for its services (certificate of insurance on file); contacts leads within [1 business hour / same day]; treats customers professionally; tells us if it can't take a lead so we can reassign it.
7. **No guarantees.** We don't guarantee a number of leads, jobs or revenue.
8. **Independent businesses.** Partner performs and quotes the work under its own agreement with the customer. We're not responsible for the work.
9. **Data.** Partner uses lead details only to serve that customer and doesn't sell or share them.
10. **Term.** Month to month. Either side can end it with 15 days' written notice. Referral fees for jobs from leads already sent still apply.
