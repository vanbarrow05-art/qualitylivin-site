# 6. Launch promo: "Founding Partners" (through Jan 31, 2027)

The launch promo ties the provider offer to the first **print** edition of the magazine (see `magazine/PRINT-LAUNCH-PLAN.md`), so it gives providers a reason to decide now and gives the magazine its first advertisers.

## For providers: Founding Partner offer

> **Become a Founding Partner of Quality Living.**
> Start with 3 free leads, as always. Founding Partners who choose a monthly plan by **December 15, 2026** also get:
> - **A feature in the first print edition** of Quality Living Magazine (Winter 2027): full page on Scale & Media, half page on AI Autopilot & Growth, a directory listing on Directory Presence
> - **Their plan price locked for 12 months**
> - **A "Founding Partner" badge** on their map listing
>
> Founding spots are limited to **[5] per service category per city**.

**Why these perks:** they cost little in cash (a magazine page you're printing anyway, a price you weren't raising yet), they create urgency (the print deadline is real), and the per-city cap rewards the best local provider for moving first.

**Before announcing, confirm you can deliver:** print pages available (a 32-page issue has room for ~8–10 partner features), the Dec 15 date works with the print schedule, and you're comfortable locking prices for a year. These become promises once they're on the website.

**Script add-on (use in Script A after the free-leads offer):**
> "One more thing: we're printing our first physical issue of the magazine for January. Founding Partners who pick a plan by December 15th get featured in it and lock their price for a year. I can tell you more after your first leads come in."

## For customers: launch offer (optional)

> **Launch special: free virtual estimate + a free copy of Quality Living Magazine.**
> Request an estimate by Jan 31, 2027 and we'll send you the digital edition today and the first print issue when it's released (while supplies last).

This builds the print subscriber list from day one. Add a checkbox "Send me the print magazine" with a mailing-address field to the GHL estimate form.

## Where the promo shows on the website

The provider section of the homepage has a "Founding Partner" banner (see `src/page.html`, search for `ql-founding`). Edit the dates and spot count there if you change them here. Remove it after Jan 31, 2027.
