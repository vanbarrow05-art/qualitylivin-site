Standards

Our Standards

Our planned approval process considers provider identity, relevant services, coverage, references, and applicable supporting documents. A published profile must accurately state what was reviewed and when. Paid advertising does not purchase a passing quality review.

  • Provider identity, services, and service-area coverage are reviewed before publication.
  • Profiles should show the review source (when provided) and the date evidence was checked.
  • Sponsored placement (when used) is labeled separately from approval status.
