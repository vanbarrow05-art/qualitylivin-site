How it works

From request to estimate in four steps

    Describe your project

    The service, property type, location and the timing you have in mind.

    Add photos & video

    For cleaning, three photos and a short walkthrough video help the provider see the scope.

Our standards

Approval is earned, never bought

Before a provider is published, our team reviews who they are and what they do. Every profile states what was checked, where the information came from, and when.

What we review

Business identity, the services offered, the areas actually covered, references, and any licenses or insurance documents that apply to the work.

Where it comes from

Ratings and reviews are shown with their source and the date we checked them. We don't publish ratings we can't trace.

What approval means

Approval means the review was completed. Sponsored placement is labeled separately, and a paid membership never changes an approval decision.
